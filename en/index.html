About Us

We apply mathematics and software engineering to solve technical problems and help companies understand the complex systems they rely on.

We provide expertise in mathematics and software development to automate time consuming manual processes and optimize the computational algorithms their work relies on.

We have applied these skills to support the United States Department of Defense, to automate a 6 hour manual data labelling task, and create visualizations of brain activity.

With experience ranging from fundamental research through software development we can easily integrate with any team in the organization and effectively communicate with stakeholders of all levels of technical knowledge.
