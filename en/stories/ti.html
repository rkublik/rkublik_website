A Place for Everything

We had a client who is pushing the boundaries of materials science research and analysis. Our client uses electron microscopes and lasers to see the microstructure of titanium alloys, and has created analysis tools to help understand how the microstructure affects the strength and longevity of the jet engine components made from these alloys.

Our client's company grew out of an academic research group, and had a collection of academic tools - the kind that get the job done, but aren't very easy to use. These tools existed as command-line utilities with multiple inputs and limited documentation that could only be invoked by the person who created them. In addition the raw data and analysis artifacts were stored across multiple computer hard drives.

We helped our client by creating a web-based platform for these tools and set up a host server on their local network. We created user-friendly web applications for each of the original command-line tools and helped to expand their capabilities by creating additional processing and visualization applications. The web platform, centralized database, and file store provided easy access to everyone in the company.

The results of our work were immediately visible. Improved data collection and storage workflows freed the team for other tasks. New visualization tools made it ebay for the CEO to review the results and share them with client teams. The main tool developer was also freed from the task of running analysis jobs, and could focus on creating new capabilities.
